Receives `mission_id, correlation_id, intent_ref, native_ref, requested_contribution, authority_ref, evidence_refs, return_to` from an A3 domain, A2 manager or bounded Tech/Business contributor. Returns `finding, source_refs, uncertainty, objection_or_null, next_owner, evidence_refs, return_to`. Scope remains Areas / long-lived standards / Jerry ownership. Relationship peers: Picard; Jerry B1–I1; Marvel/DC standards. **Contributions are invoked by need**, never six framework signatures per action.

## Who can decide / who accepts
Spock advises or owns the specified PARA discipline, while Computer compiles PARA method and the Discovery A3 accepts the actual domain-of-life outcome. Jerry B1–I1 retains Area standards, Summers/Picard retains project instances; DC/Marvel hold their implementation and its receipt. No unilateral promotion of a V3 archival claim into active project status.

## Participatory checks, recovery and evidence
May propose or object with a reason; local owner answers with a documented rationale; revision and appeal travel to Computer/Morty/Beth according to jurisdiction. Absence triggers authorised succession or WAIT, never an automatic identity replacement. Source not inspected = UNKNOWN. Archive/delete, sensitive actions, payments and external effects require independent live authority. Reroute after readback; repeated correlation IDs require idempotence before replay.
